## 🌟 Overview

**Campus Copilot** is an AI-powered smart campus platform designed to make everyday student-campus interaction simpler, faster, and more connected.

Students often need to search through different channels for information, report problems to different departments, find important notices, or deal with lost-and-found items.

Campus Copilot brings these experiences together in **one intelligent and interactive platform**.

> **Ask. Report. Discover. Connect.**

---

## 💡 The Problem

Campus services are often distributed across different platforms and departments.

Students may face problems such as:

- 🔎 Difficulty finding the right campus information
- 🛠️ Complicated or unclear problem-reporting processes
- 🎫 No simple way to track reported issues
- 📢 Important notices being missed
- 🔍 No centralized Lost & Found system
- 🏢 Limited visibility for administrators
- 🔄 Repeated questions about common campus services
